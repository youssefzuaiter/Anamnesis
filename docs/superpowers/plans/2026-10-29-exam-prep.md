# Exam-prep planner Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On a course page, pick an assessment and get review sessions spread evenly over the days before it, placed in free gaps; confirm, and they become planned tasks.

**Architecture:** A pure planner (`lib/exam-prep.ts`) reuses `freeIntervals` from `lib/day-planner.ts`. A service proposes, then on confirm re-derives the proposal on the server and creates one task per session that matches what the browser sent back (same shape as Plan my day, `plan-service.ts`). A session is an ordinary `Task` with a planned block, so the calendar, Today and Plan my day already understand it. One additive migration, a nullable `Task.assessmentId`, lets a second proposal count what is already planned.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16 server components and Server Actions, Prisma 7, Postgres, Zod, Tailwind (Quiet Studio tokens). No new dependency, no model call.

**Spec:** ROADMAP item 5, and the approved design: https://claude.ai/artifact/AHMnyhsKXSKudpgpyWnQMF (four boards: course page, plan proposed, dark with a shortfall, phone). Decisions made while shaping it, binding on this plan: sessions never go on the assessment's own day or today; the window is tomorrow up to the day before; at most 21 days back; evenly spread, one a day when there is room and at most two a day only when there are not enough days; the first free gap that fits; a session is never shortened; review time is chosen from 3 / 6 / 10 h (default 6) and session length from 45 / 60 / 90 min (default 60); the migration is a column, not a tag convention (council run inline, so weaker than independent voices: the tag approach cannot tell a course's midterm from its final and puts an id into the tag list).

## Global Constraints

- No paid service, no new dependency, no model: arithmetic only, like `lib/day-planner.ts`.
- `lib/exam-prep.ts` has no React and no Prisma import, so it is unit-testable.
- Migration is additive and nullable only: the previous release must keep working (CLAUDE.md "Operations"). Nothing is dropped or renamed.
- Every query is scoped by the session's `userId`; a client-sent id is ownership-checked by a scoped lookup (CLAUDE.md "Security").
- The proposal writes nothing. Confirming re-derives the plan on the server; times sent from the browser are only compared with it, never trusted.
- Constants, exact values: `EXAM_PREP_TAG = "exam-prep"`, `PREP_HOURS = [3, 6, 10]`, `SESSION_MINUTES = [45, 60, 90]`, `HORIZON_DAYS = 21`, `MAX_SESSIONS = 40`, at most 2 sessions a day, 10 minutes after a session placed in the same gap (`GAP_MINUTES` from `day-planner.ts`), day is 09:00 to 21:00 (`DAY_START_HOUR`, `DAY_END_HOUR`).
- A task's due date is the end of its session's local day (23:59:59.999), the rule "dates entered as yyyy-mm-dd are pinned to local end-of-day".
- Copy is exact: card title `Exam prep`; button `Propose a plan`; `Add {n} session(s) to my calendar`; `Cancel`; status `Added {n} session(s) to your calendar. They show on Today and the calendar as planned blocks.`; task title `Review: {assessment} ({i} of {n})`.
- Run heavy commands with `nice -n 19`. Playwright selects by label and by `main` (CLAUDE.md "Testing UI with Playwright").

## Review Focus

1. Another account's assessment id is refused (`RESOURCE_NOT_FOUND`) and its events and tasks are never treated as busy or counted as already planned. (Task 2)
2. Confirming twice creates sessions once: the second confirm finds nothing that still matches and is refused. (Task 2)
3. A start time the browser sends that the server did not propose creates nothing; if none match, `VALIDATION_ERROR`. (Task 2)
4. An assessment due tomorrow, today, in the past, already scored or undated gives no sessions and says why, and the apply refuses it. (Tasks 1 and 2)
5. A full calendar gives zero sessions and a message, not an error; a partly full one reports how many fit. (Tasks 1 and 2)
6. Sessions already planned (not deleted) reduce the request; a fully planned assessment proposes nothing; soft-deleted sessions do not count. (Task 2)
7. No session overlaps an event or another planned block, lands outside 09:00 to 21:00, or lands on the due day or today; month and year ends are walked correctly. (Task 1)
8. Deleting an assessment keeps its sessions and clears the link. (Task 2)
9. On a phone the card fits, every chip, row and button is at least 44px, and the page does not widen. (Task 4)

  - `sessionsNeeded(hours: number, sessionMinutes: number, plannedMinutes: number): number`: `ceil((hours * 60 - plannedMinutes) / sessionMinutes)`, never below 0, capped at `MAX_SESSIONS`.
  - `type PrepSession = { start: Date; end: Date }`
  - `type PrepPlan = { sessions: PrepSession[]; windowDays: number; noGapDays: Date[] }` (`sessions` ascending by start; `windowDays` is how many days were available; `noGapDays` are window days with no free gap as long as a session, ascending, as local midnights).
  - `planExamPrep(input: { dueDate: Date; now: Date; sessions: number; sessionMinutes: number; busy: Busy[] }): PrepPlan`
  - `sessionTitle(assessment: string, index: number, total: number): string` → `Review: Midterm (2 of 6)`, the assessment name cut to 150 characters.
  - `sessionDescription(input: { assessment: string; courseCode: string; dueDate: Date; notes: string[]; noteCount: number; dueCards: number }): string`, three lines: `Review session for Midterm (CMP2003), Tue 10 Nov.`; `Notes to go through: A, B, C and 2 more.` (all names when `noteCount` is at most `notes.length`; `No notes in this course yet.` at 0); `Start with the flashcards due for this course: 14.` (`No flashcards are due right now.` at 0).

Algorithm for `planExamPrep` (the signature and tests do not determine it):
1. `examDay` is the due date's local midnight; the window is `dayBounds(now).to` (tomorrow) up to the day before `examDay`, newest first, at most `HORIZON_DAYS` days. `days[0]` is the day before the exam. No days or `sessions <= 0` gives an empty plan.
2. Each day's gaps are `freeIntervals(day, busy, now)`; a gap can take a session of `sessionMinutes` when what is left of it is at least that long. Placing takes the first such gap, starts at its cursor, and moves the cursor to the end plus `GAP_MINUTES`.
3. Pass 1, spread: place `n1 = min(sessions, days)` sessions. Target day indexes are `round(k * (days - 1) / (n1 - 1))` for `k = 0..n1-1` (just `0` when `n1` is 1). For each target take the nearest unused day (ties go to the smaller index, closer to the exam) that has room, one session per day.
4. Pass 2, stack: sessions still unplaced go on days in order `0..days-1`, a second session on a day at most, repeating until nothing more fits.
5. `noGapDays`: window days that had no gap of `sessionMinutes` before anything was placed.
